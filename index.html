06

Questions owners ask

We already use virtual assistants. Why automate?+
Keep them — for judgment calls. Automation handles the volume underneath: sorting, first replies, reminders, data entry. Most clients redeploy VA hours to showings and owner relationships instead of inbox triage. The audit shows the split in hours.
Does this work with our software?+
We build around AppFolio, Buildium, Rent Manager, and Propertyware — through their APIs where available, and through email/portal workflows where not. Compatibility is checked on day 1 of the audit, before any blueprint is written.
How do I know these savings numbers are real?+
Because they come from your operation, not a brochure. During the audit we measure your actual message volumes and time-on-task from interviews and screen recordings, apply a conservative 75% adoption discount, and show the formula behind every figure. You always get a conservative and an expected number — never a single inflated one. And if we can't find at least $25,000/year, the audit is free.
Other agencies offer free AI audits. Why pay $997?+
A free audit is a sales pitch with a cover page. Ours is underwritten: if we don't find $25,000/year in opportunities, you don't pay — and the report is vendor-neutral, so you can take it to any builder. The $997 also becomes credit if you build with us.
We're not a technical team. Who runs this after you build it?+
It runs itself; your team just handles flagged exceptions. Every build includes 2 live training sessions and recorded step-by-step SOPs. The care plan covers monitoring and fixes if you'd rather never think about it.
What does "AI answering tenants" actually say?+
Only what you approve. Replies follow your policies and templates, urgent cases escalate to humans immediately, and everything is logged. You review the exact wording during the build — nothing improvises on your brand.
